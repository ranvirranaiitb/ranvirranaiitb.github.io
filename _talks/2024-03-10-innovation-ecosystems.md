## Overview

This keynote address explored the critical factors that contribute to building resilient innovation ecosystems that can adapt to rapidly changing technological and economic landscapes.

## Key Points

- The importance of diversity and interdisciplinary connections in fostering innovation
- How strategic investment patterns shape innovation trajectories
- The role of institutional frameworks in supporting or hindering innovative activities
- Strategies for balancing competition and collaboration within innovation ecosystems

## Impact & Outcomes

The talk sparked significant discussion around policy implications for regional innovation centers and prompted several collaborative initiatives among attending organizations.
